# Roadmap
- Phase 0: application, architecture, integrity and quality foundations.
- Phase 1 (implemented): licensed acquisition, manifests, schema and temporal audits.
- Phase 2: audited development exploration, grouped validation and versioned feature engineering.
- Phase 3 (pending authorization): grouped benchmarking, tuning and probability calibration.
- Later: baseline experiments, calibrated churn estimates, SHAP, analytics,
  margin-at-risk, assumption-labeled optimization, monitoring and isolated recruiter demo.
Horizons of 30/60/90 days require evidence from each dataset; they are not guaranteed.
