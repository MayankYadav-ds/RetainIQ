# Frozen machine learning experiment protocol

Protocol snapshot-nested-v1. The executable constants in retainiq.ml.protocol pin the dataset version, development view, source and split checksums, feature schemas, seed, search space and promotion criteria. The protocol was committed before training. The first nine months define the input period and the month-twelve state is the benchmark label.

For each frozen outer training complement, make five stratified grouped role folds. Role 0 is calibration fit, role 1 is method and threshold policy selection, and roles 2–4 are the tuning core. Run three grouped inner folds in the core. Each inner fit learns its own preprocessing. Rank candidate settings by mean inner average precision. Within 0.002, use enumeration order, favoring FS01 and the smaller unweighted setting. Fit a core-only model and use its predictions for calibration and threshold policy. The policy observations were excluded from both the base estimator and the calibrator. Choose calibration by minimum policy Brier within 0.0001, preferring identity, sigmoid and then isotonic. Isotonic requires 30 calibration observations in each class. Choose the maximum F2 on the fixed grid 0.05–0.95, preferring the larger threshold on ties. Then refit the base estimator on the full outer training complement. This can shift the score distribution relative to the core-only calibrator. Use the same procedure for the final refit on all eligible development rows.

Compare majority and prevalence references and seven candidate families where supported. Compare all three feature sets with two settings per family. Use one thread. Select champion on pooled raw outer average precision, keeping candidates within 0.002 of the best, then calibrated Brier within 0.001, then prespecified family complexity order. Record actual artifact size and latency separately. No accuracy target. Promote only a complete candidate with verified serialization and finite inference after the final receipt exists.

Use original outer folds without regeneration. Use raw score for ranking and calibrated probabilities for error and decision metrics. Use group bootstrap percentile intervals with 200 deterministic replicates and skip class-degenerate draws explicitly. Report conditional uncertainty on fixed predictions. Ties in ranking use original evaluation order with no label-based ordering. Snapshot validation cannot establish customer independence or temporal generalization. The outer score is development-informed because Phase 2 examined the eligible observations.

The benchmark and final freeze inspect the holdout only as an opaque checksum and through the Phase 2 label-blind vector audit. The separate final command requires a verified frozen model and a one-shot ledger. Only the champion and prespecified non-churn and development-prevalence references are evaluated. Risk bands 0–10%, 10–25%, 25–50% and 50–100% are descriptive policy categories with inclusive lower bounds. They do not define interventions.
