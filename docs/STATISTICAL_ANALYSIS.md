# Statistical method

Use numerical summaries for integer quantities with meaningful scale. Use counts for complaint flags and plan codes. Quantiles interpolate at index (n−1)p. Standard deviations use n−1, skew uses the third standardized moment with population SD, and anomaly flags use 1.5 times IQR. Numeric histograms use at most eight equal-width integer bins. Feature distributions by fold are available as per-fold means in the local JSON. Constant predictors skip Pearson correlation and have undefined standardized effects where the pooled scale is zero. Modal share characterizes concentration.

Pearson r reports a numeric relationship on the eligible development sample. The standardized mean difference divides the signed class mean difference by the square root of the mean of the two sample variances. Numeric intervals use an unadjusted normal approximation with class-specific variances and independent rows. Categorical proportions use 95% Wilson intervals under an independent-binomial working assumption. Duplicate vectors and unobserved customer dependence can make these nominal intervals too narrow. No p-values, multiplicity claims, causal claims or model comparisons are included. These are exploratory descriptive estimates, with sample counts supplied in the output.

Independent verification reads the development source by ordinal, recomputes integer-sum Pearson correlations, quantiles, means, category frequencies, dimensions and formulas. It checks exact validation class counts and group disjointness. Float comparisons use absolute and relative tolerance 1e-10. Its holdout access is limited to opaque checksum verification.
